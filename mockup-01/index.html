Frequently Asked Questions.

What services does RWEN Construction & Interiors Private Limited provide?

We provide full-spectrum architectural design, luxury residential interior decoration, executive office space planning, 3D CAD modeling & rendering, structural MEP engineering (mechanical, electrical, plumbing), and complete turn-key renovation execution across Kerala.

How do 3D renders help in project execution?

Our photorealistic 3D CAD renders allow clients to visualize exact material choices, lighting setups, spatial flow, and furniture placement before physical work begins, ensuring zero surprises and 100% execution accuracy.
